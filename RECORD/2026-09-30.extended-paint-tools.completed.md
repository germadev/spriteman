# Work Record: Extended Paint Tools (Completed)

## Goal / Context
Expand pixel-art tools, organize them by purpose, use the layer-selection button as the sole scope control, and make grouped-frame onion skin reference the opposite endpoint.

## Tasks & Changes
- Added eyedropper beside the color palette; line, rectangle, circle, and triangle share a split button with a dropdown and a last-used shape shortcut. Shapes preview during drag and commit once on release.
- Added rectangular selection with move, copy, cut, paste, and delete on the active layer.
- Added exact color replacement and horizontal/vertical symmetry, scoped to the active layer or all layers of the current frame according to the hierarchy's select-all button.
- Disabled single-layer paint and selection gestures when all layers are selected, including Shift/Ctrl canvas actions. Auto-generated intermediate group frames reject transforms.
- Removed visible panel headings in the mounted hierarchy, paint, palette, and inspector panels.
- Grouped-frame onion skin uses the endpoint opposite the nearest endpoint, including during opacity preview.
