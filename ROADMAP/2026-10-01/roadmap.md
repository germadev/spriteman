# Spritemotion Roadmap Snapshot - 2026-10-01

## Completed Work

- Previous-frame onion skin skips automatic intermediate frames in linked frame groups, including during opacity previews.
- Canvas target and move controls are accessible by button or temporary Shift/Ctrl keys and work with active-layer pixels, layer translation, and rectangular selections.
- The layer inspector is removed from the workspace; the layer select-all control shows its All label.
- Painting and selection tools remain organized by function, with the eyedropper next to the palette and shapes available from a split button.

## Planned Work

- Add automated browser gesture tests for keyboard modifiers, touch controls, and selection movement.
- Refine narrow-screen timeline and toolbar layout.
- Continue easing controls and raster export.

## Pending & Backlog

- Define advanced brush sizes, symmetry during live painting, and storage recovery.
